Before you ask

The questions every finance lead asks us.

How long does implementation take?

Four weeks is typical: week one we map your operation, weeks two and three we tailor the system, week four you go live. Groups with more than eight entities or heavy historical migration usually run six.

What happens to our existing data?

We migrate it. Chart of accounts, opening balances, master data and as much transaction history as you want to carry over — reconciled against your last closed period before we call it done.

What does white-label actually mean here?

Your brand, your domain, your workflow. The system your team logs into carries your identity, not ours, and the fields and reports match how your company already works rather than a template.

Do you support Bahasa Indonesia?

Both languages, from day one — interface, reports and exports. Each user picks their own; the data underneath is the same.

Where does our data live?

On infrastructure hosted in Indonesia, encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily with restores tested rather than assumed. Access is role-based and scoped per entity.
