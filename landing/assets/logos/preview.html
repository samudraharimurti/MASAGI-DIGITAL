MASAGI — LOGO OPTIONS

Six original marks in the nested-diamond language of your reference, drawn from rotated squares rather than traced. Each uses currentColor, so one file serves every colourway.

The six options

CONCENTRIC
Three nested diamonds, even rhythm. Calmest of the set.
INTERLOCK
Two nested pairs that overlap and weave. Closest to your reference.
CORE
Concentric outlines around a solid centre. Survives small sizes.
LABYRINTH
Open diamonds with staggered gaps. The maze quality, drawn fresh.
DUO
Two overlapping outlines. The simplest interlock.
FRAMED
Nested diamonds inside a containing frame. Steadiest lockup.

Colourways — shown with INTERLOCK
