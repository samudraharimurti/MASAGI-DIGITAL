# MASAGI Digital — Design System

## Mission
Deliver implementation-ready design-system guidance for MASAGI Digital that applies
consistently across the marketing site, the blog, the CMS admin, and the HV/CROM
product consoles.

## Brand
- Product/brand: MASAGI Digital (MASAGI-HV, MASAGI-CROM)
- URLs: masagi.io · blog.masagi.io · cms.masagi.io · hv.masagi.io · cro.masagi.io
- Audience: finance directors and owner-operators of Indonesian SMEs and
  multi-entity groups; secondarily their finance and admin teams
- Product surface: marketing site, editorial blog, and finance product consoles
- Languages: English and Bahasa Indonesia, always at parity

## Style Foundations

Tokens are the contract. `landing/tokens.css` is the single source of truth and is
served to every surface (`/tokens.css` on masagi.io, `/static/tokens.css` on the
blog). Components MUST consume semantic tokens; raw hex in component CSS is a bug.

- Typeface: `Inter` — `font.family.stack = "Inter", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`
- Headings: weight **600**, letter-spacing **-0.022em** (hero -0.026em), line-height 1.04–1.10
- Numerals: stat and ledger figures weight **700** with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
  In a finance product the figures MUST NOT weigh less than the prose around them.
- Body: 16–17.5px, line-height 1.6–1.65, colour `--text-2`

Type ramp (a real ramp — every step is a decision, not a measurement):
`12px · 14px · 16px · 18px · 20px · 24px · 30px · 38px · 50px`

Spacing: 4px rhythm — `4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 44 · 64 · 80`

Geometry: `--r-sm 12px` · `--r-md 20px` (cards) · `--r-lg 32px` (feature blocks) · `--pill 999px` (buttons, chips)

Colour — light is the default theme; dark is a complete equal, never an afterthought:

| Token | Light | Dark | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#FFFFFF` | `#0A0A0A` | canvas |
| `--bg-2` | `#F6F7F8` | `#141417` | alternating band |
| `--surface` | `#FFFFFF` | `#101012` | cards |
| `--border` | `#E3E6E6` | `#2C2C30` | hairline |
| `--field-border` | `#8E9294` | `#83838A` | 3.14 / 5.28 — form boundary, WCAG 1.4.11 |
| `--text` | `#0A0A0A` | `#F2F2F2` | 19.80 / 17.68 |
| `--text-2` | `#5F6165` | `#8F8F94` | 5.83 / 6.15 |
| `--text-3` | `#6E7276` | `#828289` | 4.85 / 5.19 |
| `--accent` | `#1E7A4C` | `#57D496` | 5.33 / 10.62 |
| `--accent-ink` | `#FFFFFF` | `#05140B` | 5.33 / 10.13 on the accent fill |

The accents are the MASAGI mark's own greens (`--green-1/2/3` = `#1E7A4C · #31A96A · #57D496`).
Light MUST use the deep green: `#57D496` on white is 1.9:1.

Motion: `120ms` micro-feedback (press), `180ms` hover and colour, `600ms` carousel.
All motion MUST respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility
- Target: **WCAG 2.2 AA**
- Keyboard-first interactions required.
- Focus-visible rules required — a visible ring on every interactive element.
- Contrast constraints required, and verified by computation rather than by eye.

## Writing Tone
Concise, confident, implementation-focused. Plain English and plain Bahasa; no
enterprise filler. Claims must be backed by something on the page.

## Rules: Do
- Use semantic tokens, not raw hex values in component guidance.
- Every component must define required states: default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, error.
- Responsive behavior and edge-case handling should be specified for every component family.
- Accessibility acceptance criteria must be testable in implementation.
- Every string on a public surface must exist in both `en` and `id`.
- Grid and flex children that can contain wide content must set `min-width: 0`.
- Versioned asset URLs must be stamped in the source file, not patched after deploy.

## Rules: Don't
- Do not allow low-contrast text or hidden focus indicators.
- Do not introduce one-off spacing or typography exceptions.
- Do not use ambiguous labels or non-descriptive actions.
- Do not declare tokens in more than one file; equal-specificity redeclaration makes load order decide the palette.
- Do not fabricate evidence: no invented logos, no placeholder wordmarks dressed as customers, no figures attributed to a real company.
- Do not ship a CTA that promises something the destination does not deliver.

## Guideline Authoring Workflow
1. Restate design intent in one sentence.
2. Define foundations and tokens.
3. Define component anatomy, variants, and interactions.
4. Add accessibility acceptance criteria.
5. Add anti-patterns and migration notes.
6. End with QA checklist.

## Required Output Structure
- Context and goals
- Design tokens and foundations
- Component-level rules (anatomy, variants, states, responsive behavior)
- Accessibility requirements and testable acceptance criteria
- Content and tone standards with examples
- Anti-patterns and prohibited implementations
- QA checklist

## Component Rule Expectations
- Include keyboard, pointer, and touch behavior.
- Include spacing and typography token requirements.
- Include long-content, overflow, and empty-state handling.
- Include both themes; a component is not done until it is correct in dark and light.
- Include the bilingual case: Indonesian strings run roughly 15–20% longer than English.

## Quality Gates
- Every non-negotiable rule must use "must".
- Every recommendation should use "should".
- Every accessibility rule must be testable in implementation.
- Prefer system consistency over local visual exceptions.
- Contrast claims must state the computed ratio and the pair it was measured against.
- No horizontal page scroll at 320px, 375px and 768px.
